Valuation — the framework, not a target

Multiples shown against the growth that carries them · growth-adjusted ~0.4–0.5x through peak, trough and today

Machine labor cohort — EV / next-4Q revenue and revenue growth, Q4 2020 → today

Growth-adjusted, the whole way: 2021 peak 42x ÷ ~80% ≈ 0.5x · trough 10x ÷ ~25% ≈ 0.4x · today 16.5x ÷ ~32% ≈ 0.5x. The multiple has always tracked growth — which is why value is framed in growth ranges, not point multiples. Hollow point = consensus NTM at latest marks.

L2 · State — multiple vs growth

Growth turned up (13→20%), the multiple didn't — ~0.26x growth-adjusted, half the cohort's.

L3 · Work — multiple vs growth

Steady ~20%+ growth, a de-rating multiple — ~0.31x growth-adjusted. The audit mandate is not yet priced as a meter.

What the tape pays for growth — the 67-name universe, in regimes

Median EV/NTM revenue by consensus growth band. The multiple is a step-function, not a slope: past ~30% growth the tape triples what it pays. Which band a name occupies is the only multiple question that matters.
